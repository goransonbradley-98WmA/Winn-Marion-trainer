LOTO practical · Prototype

Desktop and mobile — no headset required. Simulated equipment only.

Inspect the MCC and identify the correct disconnect.

  1. Notify affected personnel and identify all energy sources
  2. Stop equipment normally
  3. Open correct disconnect
  4. Apply personal lock and tag
  5. Check stored energy and try-start
  6. Prove meter on known live source
  7. Test all relevant conductors for absence of voltage
  8. Reprove meter on known live source

Click equipment in the 3D scene to inspect it. Drag one finger to orbit, two fingers to pan or pinch to zoom; mouse drag and wheel work on computers. Meter testing requires an open cabinet. Training logic is simplified and must be reviewed by a qualified instructor before formal use.
